Notify is the open-source notification infrastructure for sending, receiving, and tracking notifications across in-app, email, SMS, push, and chat channels. Use [workflows](/platform/concepts/workflows) to orchestrate delivery, the [Inbox component](/platform/inbox) for real-time in-app notifications, and [60+ provider integrations](/platform/integrations) for external channels.

New to Notify? Start with [What is Notify?](/platform/what-is-notify) or add the Inbox to your app with the [Next.js quickstart](/platform/quickstart/nextjs).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Notify?">
    Notify is the open-source notification infrastructure for sending, receiving, and tracking notifications across in-app, email, SMS, push, and chat channels. You define workflows, manage subscribers, and deliver messages through 60+ provider integrations or the built-in Inbox component.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What channels does Notify support?">
    Notify supports in-app (Inbox), email, SMS, push, and chat notifications. Each channel connects to third-party providers such as SendGrid, Twilio, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Slack through Notify integrations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Notify open source?">
    Yes. Notify is fully open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/novuhq/novu). You can use Notify Cloud or [self-host Notify](/community/self-hosting-notify/overview) on your own infrastructure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
